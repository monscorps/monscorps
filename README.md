### UN for Men Too · unformentoo.org

An open register of what gets tracked, funded and measured, built in
public under one discipline: every figure links its primary source,
every correction is published dated the same day it is made, and a
documented absence is recorded as absence, never zero.

- [The machinery](https://unformentoo.org/machinery.html): instruments and outcomes, side by side
- [The gap, in one picture](https://unformentoo.org/gap.html): what has machinery and what has none
- [Every receipt](https://unformentoo.org/sources.html): the public register of claims, kept and refused

Contributions arrive as data with sources, in any direction, and are
validated against the primary before anything is published. Finds are
ranked from bronze echo to meteorite, and the rarest find is the one
that proves the register wrong.
