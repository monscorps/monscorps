03 Challenge 01 Where it breaks

The scope was written by the people who don’t take the calls.

Four chairs were filled. Four were empty, and the empty ones hold the operational truth: what actually gets asked, when it spikes, what a repeat call costs, and what legal will not let a bot say.

310,000 calls a month, and nobody in the room had heard one.
