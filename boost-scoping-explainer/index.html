01  Where we are

The scope exists. The evidence doesn't.

Fifteen voice use cases were selected in a single workshop with the digital team. The people who take the calls were not in the room, and sales has already said "significant automation in year one" out loud.

Two years in

Chat works

A live channel, real containment, real logs. The expansion has a foundation — it is not a greenfield bet.

One workshop

No reps in the room

Scope was written by people who don't hear the calls. Every use case is a guess about somebody else's day.

Said, not signed

A verbal promise

"Significant automation in year one" is now the customer's expectation — with no number attached to it.

03  Where this moment actually breaks

A scope priced on a number nobody owns.

Break 01

41% is not a use case

"General enquiries" is a routing failure, not an intent. Four in ten calls cannot be designed for until somebody listens to a sample and labels them.

Break 02

15 use cases, one workshop

Breadth without depth. Fifteen half-built flows land worse than four that close the loop — and they multiply the integration surface before signature.

Break 03

NOK 42 with no source

The whole business case rests on it. If the real figure is 78, we under-promised; if it's 21, year one fails on arithmetic we agreed to in advance.

The risk is not that voice won't work. It is that success gets measured against a sentence said in a sales meeting.

04  What good looks like

Four use cases that close. Not fifteen that open.

Start here

After hours first

22% of calls meet a recorded message. Anything answered there is pure gain — no deflection argument, no agent displaced.

Then

Label the 41%

A two-week listening sample with contact centre reps turns the biggest unknown into the next three use cases, chosen by volume.

Before signature

One agreed baseline

Cost per call, containment definition and the measurement window written down and countersigned — so year one is scored against a shared number.

06  The move

Narrow the scope, widen the evidence.

Ship four voice use cases anchored on after-hours volume, label the 41% with the people who hear it, and sign one baseline before anyone signs the contract.

Outcome

A scope that survives month three

Fewer flows, each one closing an intent end to end, measured against a number both sides put their name on.
